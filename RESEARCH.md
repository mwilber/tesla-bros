# Roadster research → gameplay

Research date: October 18, 2026.

The Roadster concept uses **cold-gas thrusters**: stored compressed gas escapes through nozzles to produce short bursts of force. Tesla's October 2026 presentation and follow-up material describe rear thrusters for acceleration and a perimeter array on the Rocket configuration for upward acceleration and rotation. Tesla also describes carbon-fiber-overwrapped pressure vessels storing the compressed gas. These are manufacturer descriptions, not independently verified production specifications.

Sources consulted:

- [Tesla Roadster product page](https://www.tesla.com/roadster) — the manufacturer's current product and trim information. Its text available to the research tool was limited to the Rocket/Thrust availability notes.
- [Tesla's thruster and pressure-vessel post](https://x.com/Tesla/status/2110904290743402880) and [configuration post](https://x.com/Tesla/status/2110905588402295283) — primary statements linked and reproduced by the report below. Direct X retrieval returned a 403, so their wording was checked through that report.
- [Electrek's October 15 unveiling report](https://electrek.co/2026/10/15/tesla-unveils-cyberfied-new-roadster-kickflip/) — reproduces and links Tesla's posts about the thruster configurations, stored gas, and controls.
- [Associated Press, October 16](https://apnews.com/article/7d5f19665435b7408025bfb7a2f2f0b7) — context on the demonstrated cold-gas thrusters, suction system, and short aerial maneuvers.

## What the game borrows

- **Vertical thrust:** jumping is a brief downward gas burst that lifts the car.
- **Variable burst:** holding jump keeps the thrusters active briefly, so a tap produces a smaller hop.
- **Finite pressure:** the telemetry meter drops during a jump; jet visuals are pale white rather than a combustion flame.
- **Recharge:** solid-ground contact restores pressure quickly so the player can keep platforming.

Jump height, gas consumption, immediate ground recharge, Shift boost, and the physics are **arcade inventions**. They are not predictions of the real vehicle's performance or operating instructions. The pixel Roadster uses the supplied demo references: a low silver wedge, swept dark glazing, black lower bodywork, large turbine-style wheels, and a thin red tail light. A small faceted model rotates around its longitudinal (nose-to-tail) axis for one barrel roll per jet jump. It is a stylized arcade interpretation of the prototype.
